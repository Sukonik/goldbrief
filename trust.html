Trust / Public interest

Trust is earned.
Not advertised.

We welcome AI's potential but demand evidence, transparent limitations and open correction processes.

Editorial policy

Our standards.

Company claims are labeled

A company's announcement shows what it said, not necessarily what an independent auditor proved. We distinguish company claims, outside reporting and unreviewed automatic headlines.

No invented growth metrics

Users, downloads, paid accounts, valuations and revenue are different measurements. Rankings use transparent definitions; unavailable data is shown as unavailable.

Automatic headlines are not fact-checking

The 8:30 AM ET compilation reproduces external RSS links and titles without adding unsupported summaries. The time is approximate, not a service-level guarantee.

Human review still matters

Privacy, jobs, safety and misinformation deserve nuanced discussion. We do not assume AI companies always tell the truth or always mislead.

Corrections & independence

Open a correction issue on GitHub ↗. Any future paid sponsorship must be clearly labeled. GoldBrief is independent and not affiliated with Mozilla.
