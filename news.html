The Financial Times reported that OpenAI has cut the target for its latest funding round by $10 billion, to about $20 billion, with a desired valuation of $1.25 trillion, down from $1.4 trillion, and that the first close is expected to raise less than $20 billion after some investors reduced their allocations.

Four major labs back a shared robots.txt training opt-out ↗

OpenAI, Google, Microsoft and Anthropic said they will honor a single robots.txt token, __usage_ai-training, letting a site state in one place that its content should not be used for generative AI training. Timelines differ: Anthropic says it has started, OpenAI says in the coming months, Microsoft targets February 1, 2027, and Google says 2027.

Why it matters: One shared signal could simplify life for publishers, but it is voluntary, it does not cover search or AI answers, and it does not remove content already collected.

Independent reportAIWeekly · OpenAI
