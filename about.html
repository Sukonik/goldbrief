Mission / GoldBrief

Help people
understand AI.

Less fear, fewer distortions and more practical knowledge. AI should be understandable to everyone.

Why we exist

Open and useful by design.

GoldBrief is an open-source education and reporting experiment inspired by Mozilla's public-interest values and the GoldRang3r editorial format. It combines a linked news river, AI company trackers, markets, AI apps and learning resources.

We believe excitement and skepticism can coexist. The site aims to show what AI makes possible while clearly explaining what it gets wrong.

Help improve it

Source corrections, missing AI companies, accessibility fixes and examples of practical AI use are welcome.
