Anthropic's path to a listing, from its SEC filings.

Each stage is a different legal and market status. Dates are SEC filing dates unless noted.

  1. 2026-06-01. Draft registration statement submitted confidentially (Form DRS); confidential amendments followed through October 9
  2. 2026-10-26. Public registration statement filed (Form S-1)
  3. 2026-11-02, 11-10, 11-17. Amendments filed (Form S-1/A), including the price range on November 10 (reported by TechCrunch)
  4. 2026-11-17. Exchange registration of the stock filed (Form 8-A12B)
  5. 2026-11-18. Registration declared effective; offering priced at $83.00
  6. 2026-11-19. Final prospectus filed (Form 424B4); shares began trading on Nasdaq as ANT
  7. 2026-11-20. Settlement date per the pricing term sheet

Learn / IPOs

Rumor is not a listing.

A report about a possible IPO, a filed registration statement and an exchange-listed stock are three different stages. Consult SEC EDGAR ↗ before acting.
